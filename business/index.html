What it is

A guest-facing drink finder for restaurants, bars, and lounges.

PROOF uses a short preference survey to recommend drinks people are likely to enjoy, so guests can order with confidence and teams can learn what their audience wants.

How it works

Six quick questions. One confident order.

Guests answer simple questions about taste, strength, sweetness, citrus, and style. PROOF turns those answers into drink recommendations they can act on immediately.

Our approach

Recommendations for guests. Preference intelligence for operators.

Palate-first matching

Guests are guided by what they like, not by menu knowledge they may not have.

Cleaner demand signals

Restaurants can see which flavor profiles and drink categories people are asking for.

Better experiences

A more personal recommendation makes the night smoother, more memorable, and easier to repeat.
