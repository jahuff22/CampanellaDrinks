Cocktail menus are getting better — and harder to navigate.

Guests want to try something new. But most of them don't know what clarified milk, amaro, aquavit, or fat-washed bourbon is going to taste like.

So they hesitate. They ask for a generic recommendation, or they fall back on the same order they always place.  When they do take a chance, they often end up with a drink that tastes nothing like they expected.

More than a survey.

Question design

PROOF is built on in-depth research into taste, preference measurement, psychometrics, and survey design. Each question is designed to reveal the contours of a guest's palate, not simply to ask which spirits or flavors they already know they like.

Cocktail scoring

Every drink on a restaurant's menu is evaluated with PROOF's proprietary cocktail scoring system. The platform maps guest preferences and menu items across the same set of taste dimensions, then measures the distance between those two points.

The result

A recommendation based not on popularity or broad drink categories, but on the specific relationship between one guest's preferences and the drinks actually available on your menu.

Founder & CEO

Michael Campanella

Michael Campanella is a culinarian and mixologist obsessed with exploring why people like what they like. He studied philosophy at NYU, with a focus on philosophy of mind, personality, and epistemology, and has spent seven years studying taste science as part of his broader culinary interests. He is an award winning cook, caterer, recipe developer, and the author of Theater of the Kitchen, a cookbook that teaches readers how to use taste science and creative instinct to cook without a recipe. He is a passionate mixologist and has created over 500 original craft cocktail recipes.

Co-founder & CTO

Jake Huffaker

Jake Huffaker combines a background in computer science, behavioral economics, and optimization science to understand how people make choices and how those choices can be translated into useful patterns. He studied economics and computer science at Princeton, with a focus on behavioral economics, optimization, and data-driven decision systems. He was personally mentored in clustering and personality-based marketing by Chris Kuenne, an industry leader in customer segmentation. At PROOF, Jake leads the data architecture behind the recommendation engine, palate profiles, cocktail scoring system, and restaurant analytics.
